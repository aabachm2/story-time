Once upon a time, there was a town, and in that town there was a Biergarten, and in that Biergarten there was a mouse.
Exciting his Porche with hid Hund a man took a seat in the Biergarten, the Hund saw the mouse and started barking. 
The mouse panicked, dropped his broetchen, and having no better ideas, ran into the Porsche and locked the doors!
Eventually the Hund grew hundemuede, while the mouse sped away in the Porsche with Rammstein blasting through the speakers to satisfy an incessant Ohrwurm.
The mouse drove down the Hauptstrasse so fast that he crashed into a Baeckerei, sending Sauerkraut and Apfelkuchen flying everywhere!

The mouse crawled out of the crashed Porsche with Rammstein still blaring down the Hauptstrasse and thought "This town could use a Staubsauger right now"

"How could you!" shouted the Mehlhase, as she surveyed the mess!
Just then, the bakery's frantic Mehlhase emerged from the flour cloud wearing oven Handschuhe, desperately trying to salvage any surviving Gebaeck so the family business could remain wettbewerbsfaehig.Spotting his escape route, the resourceful mouse snatched a fresh Doener off the counter, leapt onto an abandoned Drahtesel, and pedaled frantically away from the carnage.
Watching the chaotic getaway, a local Pantoffelheld dressed in a frilly Dirndel cranked the shop's Klimaanlage to full blast and muttered that this was definitely not covered by insurance. The pantoffelheld decided to grab a Staubsauger and help clean up the mess of Gebaeck and glass before somebody slipped and hurt themselves.

Unfortunately, the Staubsauger sucked up the mouse's abandoned Broetchen and exploded, covering the entire Biergarten in breadcrumbs.

For dinner we ate some Sauerkraut at the Biergarten, the we went to see Rammstein and cried because their music is so Ohrwurm.
